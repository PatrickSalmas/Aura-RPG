- General Upgrade Idea:
	Each ability has 3-5 different upgrade/augments. Each time you upgrade an ability, you can choose any of the x number of augments for that ability. This give more build variety and allows the player to be more creative as they don't have to go down as much of a linear path when creating their build
		- How to we allow the player to put points into abilities but still allow them have fun trying new builds. The more points the player puts into certain abilities, the more tied they will feel to using that build/those abilities. Of course they will have the freedom to to respec and spend their points again, but when you want to expirement that could get tedious.
			- We could just give them multiple save builds they can switch between. Do we want there to be some restictions on how often or where they can change their build? Or just say screw it and let them have fun. Sometimes a certain level of friction can actually be good for the player experience.
